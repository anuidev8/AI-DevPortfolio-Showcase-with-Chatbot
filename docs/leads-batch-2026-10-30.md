| **Spain** | ❌ | ❌ Cold commercial email is banned without consent (LSSI art. 21) | ✅ Call only, and stop if they say no |

**Rules for every market**

1. **Send by hand.** Keep to about 20 WhatsApps a day. Bulk tools and repeated "block" reports can get your number banned.
2. **Max 3 follow-ups.** If someone says stop, stop and note it in the tracker.
3. **Tell them how you got their contact:** "I found your clinic on Google Maps."
4. **Skip personal-looking emails** (`gmail`, `hotmail`, and similar). 92 leads are marked `personal-looking` in the `emailType` column. These are the most likely to be personal data under Colombia's Ley 1581. Use WhatsApp or a call for them.
5. **Never quote a bad review** in your message. Use `painSignal` only to choose who to contact first.

**Cold email status:** `getangelmateus.com` and `meetangelmateus.com` have no mail records yet, so the warmup hasn't started. The Nov 18 launch needs about 21 days of warmup, so it moves back day for day. Until then, send only a few personal emails from `contact@angelmateus.com`.

> This is general guidance, not legal advice.
