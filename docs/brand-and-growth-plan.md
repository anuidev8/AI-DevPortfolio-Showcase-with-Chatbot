### 4.2 Cold outreach (main B2B engine)

#### Setup (week 1 — start immediately, warmup takes 2–3 weeks)

1. Buy 2 outreach domains, create 2 inboxes each (4 inboxes)
2. Configure **SPF, DKIM, DMARC** on each domain
3. Connect inboxes to **Instantly**, turn on warmup (14–21 days)
4. Max **30 emails / inbox / day** → 4 inboxes = **120 emails/day**

#### Lead sources

- **Voice AI:** Outscraper / Google Maps → clinics, real estate, hotels in Medellín, Bogotá, Miami
- **Enterprise:** Apollo → CTOs, Heads of Innovation, Ops managers at companies 50–1,000 employees
- **Workshops:** Apollo → HR / L&D / Innovation managers

#### The "missed call test" + Loom (highest reply rate for Voice AI)

1. Write to the clinic's WhatsApp at 9 pm and/or call — note if anyone answers
2. Record a 60-sec Loom showing it + a demo of what the AI would have answered
3. Send by email + WhatsApp the next morning

#### Email sequence — Voice AI (4 emails)

**Email 1 (day 1)** — Subject: `patients messaging you at night`
> Hi Dr. {{lastName}}, I noticed {{clinic}} gets lots of reviews from foreign patients. Most clinics lose 30–40% of those leads because WhatsApp isn't answered after hours.
> I build AI receptionists that reply in English/Spanish in under 10 seconds and book directly in your calendar. Live in 14 days — and you don't pay the monthly until it books 15 appointments.
> Worth a 15-min call this week?
> — Angel

**Email 2 (day 3)** — Subject: `re: patients messaging you at night`
> Recorded a 60-sec demo of what your AI receptionist would sound like: {{loomLink}}

**Email 3 (day 7)** — Subject: `quick case`
> Short case study + result (appointments booked, response time). One line CTA.

**Email 4 (day 12)** — Subject: `closing the loop`
> "Should I close your file, or is this worth a look next month?"

#### Email sequence — Enterprise (shorter, more formal)

**Email 1** — Subject: `AI agents for {{company}}`
> Hi {{firstName}}, I help companies like {{company}} ship AI agents connected to their own data — support, internal ops, voice. Recent: {{caseStudy}}.
> Would a 2-week AI roadmap (what to automate first + ROI) be useful for your team?

**Email 2** — share a relevant case study / short article from `/post`
**Email 3** — offer a free 30-min "AI opportunities" call for their team

#### Cold calling (local businesses — Voice AI)

> "Hi, this is Angel, I'll be quick — I know I'm calling out of the blue. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and you tell me if it makes sense?"
> → Pain: missed calls/WhatsApp after hours → Offer: free demo → Ask: 15-min call.

### 4.4 Paid ads — Meta (Facebook + Instagram), lowest cost that works

**Why Meta and not Google/LinkedIn:** cheapest reach in LatAm, WhatsApp click-to-message ads convert well in Colombia, great for local businesses, developers, and events. LinkedIn ads are too expensive (USD 8–15/click) at this stage.

**Start only after:** 3+ demo Reels with good organic engagement.

#### Structure (USD ~5–10/day total)

```
Campaign A — Warm-up (Awareness / Video views)      USD 3/day
  └── Ad set: Colombia + LatAm, 22–55, Advantage+ audience
      └── Ads: 3 best demo Reels

Campaign B — Retargeting (Leads / WhatsApp messages)  USD 5/day
  └── Ad set: people who watched 50%+ of videos (30 days)
             + Instagram profile engagers (90 days)
      ├── Ad: Voice AI → "Try the demo" → WhatsApp
      └── Ad: Mentorship → "Free workshop" → signup form
```

#### Rules

- Test **3 creatives** at a time; after **USD 20 spend** turn off the worst one
- Target cost: **< USD 3 per WhatsApp conversation**, **< USD 5 per workshop signup**
- Reply to every WhatsApp lead in **< 5 minutes** (or let your own AI agent do it — it's also a live demo)
- Events: **USD 3–5/day for 7 days before each event** targeting Medellín, 20–45, tech/startup interests

#### Ads that work best (low cost)

1. Raw phone video of you showing the demo (no fancy production)
2. Screen recording of the AI answering WhatsApp in real time
3. Event clip with crowd + "Next AI After Hours: {{date}}"

### 4.5 Sales — the call (Hormozi CLOSER)

1. **Clarify** why they're on the call
2. **Label** the problem ("you're losing night leads")
3. **Overview** past pain ("what have you tried?")
4. **Sell the vacation** — the result, not the tech
5. **Explain away** concerns — show the live demo
6. **Reinforce** — send contract + payment link on the call
