## 3. Post formula

```
1. HOOK      → first line stops the scroll (a number, a surprise, a question)
2. STORY     → 3–5 short lines, one idea
3. LESSON    → what the reader takes away
4. CTA       → one ask: comment, DM, or "link in the first comment"
```

- **LinkedIn:** link in the **first comment**, not in the post body
- **Instagram:** "link in bio" or "comment `AGENT` and I'll DM you the demo"
- **Language:** events/Medellín → Spanish (or both); voice AI for US → English; mentorship → both
