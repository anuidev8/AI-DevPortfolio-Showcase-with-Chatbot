## 4. Networking breaks — how Daniel runs them

Each break is **10 minutes**: ~3 minutes on mic, ~7 minutes people talking in pairs/small groups.

### Break 1 (7:50 PM) — "What are you building?"

1. Daniel: *"Before the next talk — let's meet each other. Who wants to share, in 30 seconds: who are you, and what project are you building right now?"*
2. Pick **1 volunteer**. Give them the mic. Keep them to 30–60 seconds.
3. Ask one follow-up: *"And what do you need right now to move it forward?"*
4. Close it for them: *"If you can help them, or you need that — find them in the break. You have 7 minutes: turn to someone you don't know, scan their NoShy card, and ask what they're building. Go."*

### Break 2 (8:25 PM) — "What do you expect from tonight?"

1. Daniel: *"Quick check-in. Who came tonight looking for something specific — a client, a partner, a job, an answer?"*
2. Pick **1 volunteer**. Mic, 30–60 seconds.
3. Ask: *"What would make tonight a win for you?"*
4. Close: *"Who heard something they can help with? Go find that person now. 7 minutes — and remember to scan each other's cards so the connection counts. Go."*

**Tips for Daniel:** pick fast, don't wait for perfect silence · repeat the person's name and project out loud so the room can find them later · if nobody volunteers in 10 seconds, pick a friendly face: *"You, smile — what brings you here?"*
