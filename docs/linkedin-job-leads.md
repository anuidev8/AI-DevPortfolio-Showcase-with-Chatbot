# LinkedIn job-post leads — Oct 28, 2026

_Source: public LinkedIn job search (remote, US, posted in the last 7 days). Keywords: voice AI, conversational AI, agentic AI, Next.js AI, React Native health._

**The idea:** a company posting a job already has a problem it's paying to solve. Small teams with an open role often take a contractor who can **start this week** while they keep looking for a full-time hire. You're pitching *"I can ship this for you now"*, not *"please hire me"*.

**Your angle (one line):** _I build AI voice agents, AI coaches and React / React Native health & wellness apps. Remote from Medellín, on US hours, English + Spanish._

---

## How to send (10 min per lead)

1. Open the job link and read the post. Pick out **one** detail to mention.
2. On the company's LinkedIn page → **People** → search `founder`, `CTO`, `head of engineering` or `engineering manager`. Message that person, **not** the recruiter.
3. Send a **connection request with the note** (limit: 300 characters).
4. Once they accept, send **Message 2**. If there's no reply after 3 days, send **Message 3**.
5. If the post says full-time only, also **apply** through the post. The DM then backs up your application.

> ⚠️ Send these by hand. LinkedIn bans accounts for automated messaging. Keep it to about 20 connection requests a day.
